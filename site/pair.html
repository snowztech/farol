Scan this code from the Farol app

The code on your Mac pairs your iPhone with Farol, and it has to be scanned from inside the app. Your phone's camera brought you here instead.

Pair your iPhone

  1. Install Farol for iPhone from the App Store.
  2. Open it and tap Scan the code.
  3. Point it at the code in Farol's settings on your Mac, under Remote. A code lasts two minutes, so press Show code again if it has gone.

Keep this link to yourself

The address of this page holds the pairing code. Whoever has it can pair with your Mac until the code runs out, so do not share or screenshot it. This page reads nothing from it and sends it nowhere.

What it is for

With a phone paired, you see which of your agents is working, waiting for you or done, and you answer from wherever you are. Everything between your Mac and your phone is encrypted end to end. See what Farol sends and keeps.
